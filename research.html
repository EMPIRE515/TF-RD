Ongoing Project 2024 - Present

Primate Population Density & Canopy Mapping

Lead Investigators: Dr. E. Ndip, TFRD Field Biology Unit

Objective & Scope

Using a combination of bioacoustic monitoring devices and camera traps, this initiative tracks the migratory and breeding patterns of Western Lowland Gorillas and Chimpanzees across the secondary forest borders.

Current Progress

Over 140 days of continuous acoustic data collected. Preliminary observations indicate a 4% increase in group nesting frequencies near community forest edges compared to the 2023 baseline survey zones.

Field Metrics

  • Camera Traps: 42 Active Nodes
  • Data Collected: 1.2 TB Field Audio
  • Community Scouts: 12 Trained Trackers

Completed 2022 - 2025

Ethnobotanical Survey of Non-Timber Forest Products (NTFPs)

Collaborative Partners: Baka Indigenous Council & TFRD Socio-Economic Team

Final Assessment

A comprehensive classification of 85 indigenous plant species utilized by the forest-dwelling communities. The research evaluated sustainable harvest boundaries for Moabi bark and Wild Mango seeds to protect long-term flora regeneration cycles.

Key Outcomes

Successfully established three community-managed nurseries. The published findings formed the foundational extraction guidelines adopted by local sustainable cosmetics production units.

Impact Summary

  • Species Catalogued: 85 Varieties
  • Published Papers: 2 Peer-Reviewed
  • Local Income Impact: +18% Harvest Value
